# 3.0 test plan — `feature/3.0-comms`

Everything on the branch up to `0967a50` (2026-11-02): groundwork (radio source/antenna, leases, one quality
function) and workstream B (vehicle radios, intercom, infantry phone). Nothing here has been run in game yet;
each part compiles (Workbench) and the plugin passes its offline tests. Manpacks (A) and EW/relays/SATCOM (C)
are not built, so not in this list.

Tick a box when it passes; note the log time and anything odd beside it. When something fails, grab the
client and server logs and the TeamSpeak client log before retrying.

## Setup

- **Builds:** plugin `plugin\build\LimaCharlie_3.0-dev-01.ts3_plugin` (`plugin\build.bat`) on every
  client; mod from the **Lima Charlie DEV** project (not with the release mod loaded). Both must be 3.0 dev builds: protocol 10 rejects 2.0.x on either
  side.
- **Where:** dedicated or listen server + 2 clients on TeamSpeak 3 (a third helps for intercom/phone).
  Section 3 is Workbench single-player (diag menu).
- **Logs:** grep `[LC]` in the game logs (client and server). The vehicle radio spawn line is DEBUG level.
  `$profile:LimaCharlie\game_state.json` shows what the game tells the plugin (`radios[]`, `self.ic`,
  `players[].ic`).
- **Watch throughout:** script errors (`SCRIPT (E)`), frame drops when vehicles are around, and TeamSpeak
  crashes.

## 1. Start-up and versions

- [ ] Mod-loaded notice shows `3.0-dev-01` and the TeamSpeak plugin version `3.0-dev-01`.
- [ ] Mixed versions fail loudly, not silently: a 2.0.5 plugin with the 3.0 mod (and the reverse) gets no
      voice at all, and the status notice / troubleshooting symptom matches docs/troubleshooting.md
      ("Protocol mismatch").
- [ ] Two 3.0 clients: no LC warnings or errors in either log after joining.

## 2. Nothing that worked in 2.0.5 has changed (regression)

Groundwork 1 changed how radios are announced (antenna position, per-radio terrain links); it should
sound identical.

- [ ] Direct speech: whisper / normal / shout ranges, walls and doors muffle, room reverb inside buildings.
- [ ] Hand radio near and far: clear close up, garble towards max range, cut beyond it.
- [ ] Terrain: transmit from behind a hill / ridge → garbled as in 2.0.5; same spot in the open → clear.
- [ ] Worn manpack (PRC-77 / R-107 on the back): transmits and receives as before.
      `game_state.json` lists it with `"src":"pack"`; a hand radio has `"src":"held"`.
- [ ] Both transmit keys (TX1 long range, TX2 short range) and the transmit-key picker.
- [ ] Two radios on different frequencies: each heard on its own ear/volume setting.
- [ ] Game Master in the editor: unlimited range still works.
- [ ] AI hearing: AI react to shouting / radio as before.
- [ ] Ear-protection hook (only if the OTAC compat or a test caller is available): unchanged from 2.0.5.

## 3. Radio leases (Workbench single-player, diag menu)

Groundwork 2: the server decides who may use a radio that is not in their inventory. Diag menu →
*Lima Charlie*.

- [ ] Drop a manpack (or stand next to an AI radioman), tick *Lease nearest radio*:
      `[LC] Radio lease granted`, `[LC] Radio leases held: 1`; `game_state.json` lists it with
      `"src":"borrowed"` at the radio's position.
- [ ] Walk more than 4.5 m away: lease ends with `OUT_OF_RANGE`; it leaves `radios[]`.
- [ ] Untick the option: all leases released.
- [ ] Sit in the driver seat of an M151 / UAZ, tick *Lease this vehicle's radio*: granted. Move to a cargo
      seat: ends with `WRONG_SEAT`.
- [ ] Die while holding a lease: it ends.

## 4. Vehicle radios (MP, 2 clients)

LC adds a radio to military vehicles (cars and trucks military-only; APCs, tracked vehicles and
helicopters all). Crew seats use it: driver/pilot, commander, gunner.

- [ ] **Spawn (first, everything else depends on it):** place a LAV-25 or UAZ469 and play. Log has
      `[LC] Vehicle radio mounted on <vehicle>`; no `Vehicle radio prefab not found`.
- [ ] One of each class gets a radio: wheeled car (UAZ469 / M151), truck (Ural / M923), APC (BTR-70 /
      LAV-25), helicopter (UH-1H / Mi-8). A civilian car or truck has none.
- [ ] Crew seat → lease granted (`... VEHICLE`) and the vehicle radio appears in the VON menu. Cargo seat,
      getting out, or dying → lease ends and the entry goes.
- [ ] Copilot (helicopter) and commander seats count as crew; every passenger seat does not.
- [ ] Tuning: one crew member changes frequency / power; the other crew member sees the change. A cargo
      player cannot change it.
- [ ] Transmit on it from inside; a hand radio on the same frequency outside hears it, from the **vehicle's**
      position (drive away while talking: range follows the vehicle).
- [ ] Receiving: a hand radio far away transmits; the crew hear it on the vehicle radio. Range and terrain are
      measured from the vehicle's antenna.
- [ ] Encryption key: in an enemy vehicle (or a GM-placed one with no faction key) transmitting is blocked.
      Hover the radio, press Y to load your key, transmit again: works for both crew.
- [ ] Mute: one crew member mutes the vehicle radio with M; the other still hears it.
- [ ] Two vehicles on one frequency: each crew hears the other at radio range.
- [ ] Join in progress: a player who joins while a crew is seated sees the right frequency and gets the radio
      when they take a crew seat.
- [ ] Vehicle destroyed or deleted by the GM with crew inside: entries go, no script errors.

## 5. Intercom (MP, 2–3 clients)

Crew / Cargo / Misc channels per vehicle, picked on the **Intercom** entry at the end of the radio menu.

- [ ] Driver and a passenger: driver starts on Crew, passenger on Cargo. They hear each other only as
      normal direct speech (muffled by the engine).
- [ ] Passenger scrolls Intercom to Crew: now both are heard clear and centred, including a whisper with the
      engine running, whatever their voice level.
- [ ] No Intercom: off again; the passenger is back to direct speech only.
- [ ] Selecting the Intercom entry never opens a radio: the radio key still transmits on your radio.
- [ ] The label in the middle of the radial menu follows the channel name as you scroll.
- [ ] Whether the radial menu closes when a radio lease is granted while it is open (should happen at most
      once, just after sitting down); note what you see.
- [ ] Third player in a **different** vehicle on Crew: does not hear the first vehicle's intercom.
- [ ] Third player in the same vehicle (other seat): channels behave per player, as above (checks that other
      players' seats are known on every client).
- [ ] Helicopter with rotors running: crew intercom clear.
- [ ] Join in progress into a vehicle where someone already picked a non-default channel: the picks are
      correct for the joiner.
- [ ] Civilian car: no Intercom entry.

## 6. Infantry phone (BTR-70 and LAV-25 passenger door)

Where: BTR-70 right side door (`door_r01`), LAV-25 right rear door (`door_back_right`). Not at the rear
`push_rear` any more, and not at the other doors.

- [ ] On foot at the passenger door: **Pick Up Infantry Phone** shows among the door's actions (Open Door,
      Get In...); it does not show at the other doors or at the rear Push context. Log `[LC] Radio lease granted: ... PHONE`;
      the Intercom entry appears on Crew.
- [ ] Driver / commander / gunner hear you clear with no switching, and you hear them.
- [ ] Cargo on Cargo does not hear you until they switch to Crew.
- [ ] Walk more than ~5.5 m away **from the door**: ends with `OUT_OF_RANGE`; the entry goes. (Measured from
      the door, not the vehicle's centre: on the LAV-25 walk forward past the bow, it should end sooner.)
- [ ] Pick up again, then **Hang Up**: ends.
- [ ] Pick up, then get into any seat: ends with `WRONG_SEAT`.
- [ ] Pick up, then die: ends.
- [ ] Two players on the phone at once: both work.
- [ ] The action does not show from inside the vehicle, on other vehicles, or on a civilian vehicle.

Handset and cable (look from your own view and from a second client):

- [ ] Picked up: you hold the handset **like a radio in the left hand** (the AN/PRC-68 hold, not inspect)
      holding a blue handset; a thin **black** cable runs from the **bottom** of the handset to the door,
      sagging, never through the ground. Your inventory has an "Infantry Phone Handset" item while you hold it,
      gone on hang-up. Note how the handset sits (`GRIP_OFFSET` / `HANDSET_ANGLES` in LC_HandsetCable.c).
- [ ] While on the phone the world actions still show (Hang Up at the door) and there is no forced free look.
      Pulling out something else ends the pose and it is not forced back; the phone stays held.
- [ ] Drop the handset item from the inventory: the phone hangs up ("handset dropped"), nothing left on the
      ground.
- [ ] Walk around within the 5 m: the cable follows every frame, sags less as you walk away, straight near
      the limit. Get the vehicle driven slowly beside you: the far end stays on the door.
- [ ] The second client sees the same handset and cable on you; both disappear for both when you hang up,
      walk off, get in, or die. Respawn: nothing left in the world.
- [ ] Walk the second client more than 100 m away and back: the cable is gone at range, back when near.
- [ ] Two players on one phone: two cables. Log has no `SCRIPT (E)` and no `NULL pointer` mentioning
      `LC_HandsetCable`.

## Expected, not bugs

- Switching to another radio on the same frequency is briefly clear (up to 300 ms) before terrain applies:
  terrain links are per radio now.
- Leased radios from the diag menu reach `radios[]` and the transmit keys but not the radial menu (only
  vehicle radios and the phone add menu entries).
- The vehicle radio's mute resets when you change seat.
- Mod and plugin from 2.0.x cannot talk to 3.0 at all (protocol 10).
